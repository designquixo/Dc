1 Introduction & Statutory Scope

Design Quixo ("Company", "we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to safeguarding the privacy, confidentiality, and integrity of the personal data entrusted to us by our clients, website visitors, and creative partners.

This Privacy Policy sets out how we collect, store, process, transfer, and protect your Personal Information in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA), Section 43A of the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 (SPDI Rules), and applicable international data protection standards.

2 Categories of Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum personal and operational data necessary to deliver high-speed graphic design services:

  • Direct Contact & Identity Identifiers: Name, phone number, WhatsApp contact number, email address, company or business name, and billing city.
  • Project & Creative Brief Assets: Text copy, brand logos, vector files, high-resolution product photography, color palette specifications, font preferences, dimensional requirements, and creative guidance provided by you.
  • Financial & Invoicing Data: Payment transaction reference numbers, payment mode (UPI, card, net banking), billing addresses, and GSTIN (where GST invoicing is requested). Note: Design Quixo does NOT store credit/debit card numbers, CVV codes, or banking PINs on its servers. All payments are processed through PCI-DSS certified, RBI-approved Indian payment aggregators.
  • Technical & Telemetry Data: IP address, device hardware model, browser type, operating system, approximate geographic city-level location, referrer URLs, and interaction timestamps.

Your data is processed strictly based on your consent and contractual necessity for the following purposes:

  • Fulfilling Design Orders: Dispatching your design requirements to verified human graphic designers, generating proofs, and delivering high-res print and source files.

7

Cookies & Tracking Technologies Policy

A cookie is a small alphanumeric text file placed on your device by your web browser when you visit Design Quixo. Cookies allow our platform to recognize your browser, maintain active sessions, remember your design draft inputs, and ensure smooth, responsive website performance.

1. Strictly Necessary / Essential Cookies (Always Active)

These cookies and browser local storage tokens are essential for the operation of Design Quixo. They enable secure authentication, maintain your design order draft while filling forms, prevent CSRF attacks, and verify legitimate browser sessions. Without these cookies, our core services cannot function.

2. Functional & Preference Cookies

These cookies remember your preferences—such as your selected city, recent service category lookups, language choices, and cookie consent status—so you don't have to re-enter them on subsequent visits.

3. Performance & Diagnostic Telemetry

We collect anonymous, aggregated technical metrics regarding page loading speeds, screen responsiveness, and error logs to continually optimize platform speed and fix mobile layout issues.

How You Can Control and Manage Cookies:

Most modern web browsers allow you to accept, block, or delete cookies via your browser preferences:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data.
  • Apple Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions.

Please note that disabling strictly necessary cookies may degrade certain core interactive features such as instant brief submission or designer login.

8 Your Rights Under the DPDPA 2023

As a Data Principal under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you possess the following statutory rights:

  • Right to Access: You have the right to request a summary of the personal data we hold about you and the processing activities undertaken.
  • Right to Correction & Updating: You may request the correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to Be Forgotten): You may request the permanent deletion of your personal data when it is no longer required for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to legal retention mandates under tax laws.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal: You have the right to lodge a formal complaint with our designated Grievance Officer and escalate to the Data Protection Board of India if unresolved.
  • Right to Nominate: You have the right to nominate an individual to exercise your data rights in the event of death or incapacity.

9 Data Retention & Deletion Schedule

We retain your design files and transaction details for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide you with re-download access to previous deliverables. Billing records and GST invoices are preserved for a period of up to 7 years in compliance with Section 36 of the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017.

Upon your formal written request to our Compliance Support Desk, all identifiable marketing and personal records not required for statutory compliance will be permanently purged within thirty (30) calendar days.
