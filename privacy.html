2 Categories of Data Principals & Personal Data Collected

In accordance with Section 5 of the DPDP Act, we collect only the minimum personal data strictly necessary to deliver high-speed manual graphic design services. We categorize data into two distinct Data Principal streams:

Your Statutory Rights Under the DPDP Act 2023 & Rules 2025/2026

As a Data Principal (whether a client ordering designs or a creator registered on our platform), you enjoy the following statutory rights under Chapter III of the DPDP Act:

1. Right to Access (Section 11)

You may request a summary of the personal data being processed about you, categories of third parties with whom data has been shared, and any other processing information.

2. Right to Correction (Section 12)

You have the right to request correction, updating, or completion of any inaccurate, misleading, or outdated personal data held by us.

3. Right to Erasure (Section 12)

You may request the permanent deletion of your personal data when it is no longer necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject only to statutory tax retention mandates.

4. Right to Nominate (Section 14)

You have the statutory right to nominate another individual who shall exercise your data rights in the event of death or incapacity.

5. Right to Withdraw Consent

You may withdraw your consent at any time as easily as giving consent. Upon withdrawal, we cease processing your personal data unless required by statutory law.

6. Right to Grievance Redressal (Section 13)

You have the right to lodge a formal complaint with our designated Grievance Officer, and escalate unresolved complaints to the Data Protection Board of India.

8 Children's Privacy Protection (Section 9 DPDP Act)

Design Quixo adheres strictly to Section 9 of the DPDP Act 2023/2026:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal data from or onboard individuals under eighteen (18) years of age without verifiable parental consent.
  • We do not undertake tracking, behavioral monitoring, or targeted advertising directed at children or minors.
  • If we learn that personal data of a child has been collected without verified parental authorization, we take immediate steps to permanently delete such information from our records.

9 Data Retention & Deletion Schedule

We retain your design files and transaction details for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide you with re-download access to previous deliverables. Billing records and GST invoices are preserved for a period of up to 7 years in compliance with Section 36 of the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017.

Upon your formal written request to our Compliance Support Desk, all identifiable marketing and personal records not required for statutory compliance will be permanently purged within thirty (30) calendar days.

Cookies & Tracking Technologies Policy

A cookie is a small alphanumeric text file placed on your device by your web browser when you visit Design Quixo. Cookies allow our platform to recognize your browser, maintain active sessions, remember your design draft inputs, and ensure smooth, responsive website performance.

1. Strictly Necessary / Essential Cookies (Always Active)

These cookies and browser local storage tokens are essential for the operation of Design Quixo. They enable secure authentication, maintain your design order draft while filling forms, prevent CSRF attacks, and verify legitimate browser sessions. Without these cookies, our core services cannot function.

2. Functional & Preference Cookies

These cookies remember your preferences—such as your selected city, recent service category lookups, language choices, and cookie consent status—so you don't have to re-enter them on subsequent visits.

3. Performance & Diagnostic Telemetry

We collect anonymous, aggregated technical metrics regarding page loading speeds, screen responsiveness, and error logs to continually optimize platform speed and fix mobile layout issues.
